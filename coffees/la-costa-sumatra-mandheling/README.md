## What to Expect

A classic Indonesian wet-hulled Sumatran: full body, low acidity, and earthy depth over brightness. Giling Basah processing leaves the parchment on during drying, creating that distinctive musty-sweet, herbal character — expect dark chocolate, spice, and brown sugar rather than fruit or florals.

- **Origin character:** Sumatran Mandheling is known for heavy, syrupy body and earthy intensity — less about acidity, more about texture and depth
- **Wet-hulled processing:** The Giling Basah method imparts fermented, earthy notes and a heavier mouthfeel than washed; lower pressure preserves the sweet dark-chocolate character without extracting the harder, more astringent compounds
- **Density/Altitude:** Moderate altitude (1100–1600 masl) means less bean density than high-altitude East Africans — the puck may offer less resistance, so watch your shot time and be ready to go finer if it runs fast
