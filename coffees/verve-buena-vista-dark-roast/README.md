## What to Expect

A direct-trade dark roast blend that aims to keep some clarity despite the 8/10 roast level. The Latin American base supplies the chocolate-and-maple backbone, while the East African component is likely what's giving the red apple note — a tart, juicy lift that's easy to lose if extraction goes hot or hard. Aim for gentle extraction to keep all three notes legible.

- **Origin character:** Colombia provides chocolate, body, and caramel sweetness; East African (likely Ethiopian or Kenyan) contributes the brighter red apple acidity that lifts the cup.
- **Processing (washed):** Cleaner cup, more articulate notes — supports a slightly longer ratio (1:2 vs the library's 1:1.5-2 for darks).
- **Roast level (8/10):** Past second crack territory. Oils may be present on the bean surface. Susceptible to ash/smoke if over-extracted — keep pressure and temperature conservative.
