## What to Expect

Tokyo Kissa is Blue Bottle's darkest production blend, designed to evoke the deep, resonant roasts of traditional Japanese kissaten coffeehouses. Past second crack with likely visible oils on the bean. The marshmallow/vanilla notes are delicate top-end sweetness that will be the first thing lost to over-extraction — restraint is the design goal.

- **Origin character:** Latin American base supplies the bittersweet cocoa backbone and body; the washed Ethiopian component is what's giving structure and any lift in the cup.
- **Processing (washed):** Cleaner, more articulate at this roast depth than a natural would be — supports a slightly longer ratio (1:2) than the library's 1:1.5-2 baseline for darks.
- **Roast level (darkest BB blend):** More soluble, more bitter compounds available, lower density. Cooler temp, coarser grind, lower peak pressure relative to a typical "8/10" dark.
