## What to Expect

Belle is Klatch's flagship espresso blend — a 94-point Coffee Review pick built for chocolate-forward, milk-friendly drinking. The Cerrado Mineiro origin and medium-dark roast push the cup toward sweetness and body rather than bright acidity, with subtle dark-fruit and boozy notes hiding underneath the chocolate.

- **Origin character:** Brazilian Cerrado typically delivers low-acidity, chocolate/nut-forward profiles with a heavy body — ideal for milk drinks and traditional espresso lovers.
- **Processing (likely natural/pulped natural):** Adds the blackberry and brandy notes on top of the Brazilian chocolate base. Naturals extract a bit faster than washed at the same grind, so expect to dial slightly finer than washed med-darks if shots run short.
- **Roast level:** Medium-dark wants lower pressure and slightly lower temp than a typical medium — pushes sweetness, avoids ash/bitterness.
