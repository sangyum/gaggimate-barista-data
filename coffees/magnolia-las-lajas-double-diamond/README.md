## What to Expect

A boundary-pushing Costa Rican from one of the country's most inventive processing families. The two-stage anaerobic natural amplifies fruit and adds a wine-like intensity while retaining Central Valley clarity — this is fruit-forward espresso with real backbone, not funky-for-funk's-sake.

- **Origin character:** Costa Rica Central Valley usually leans clean, balanced, and bright. Las Lajas takes that baseline and layers on fermented fruit complexity through processing.
- **Variety (Sarchimor):** A Timor-hybrid cross bred for disease resistance — historically flavor-limited, but Las Lajas' processing extracts remarkable expression from it. Expect medium body, decent acidity.
- **Processing (Double Anaerobic Natural):** Sealed oxygen-free fermentation twice over — transforms sugars into acids and aromatic esters. Gives boozy/winey/tropical intensity. Wants a long ratio and gentle pressure to unpack without collapsing into ferment funk.
- **Altitude (1,600 m):** Mid-altitude density — not as brittle as the 1,800m+ ultra-lights, so grind won't need to be brutally fine.
