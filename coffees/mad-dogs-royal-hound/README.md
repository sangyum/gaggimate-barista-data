## What to Expect

A standing medium-roast offering sourced through Dieseldorff Kaffee, a small-batch Guatemalan importer. Atitlán coffees are known for clean citrus brightness and good body from the volcanic lake-region soil — expect clarity over complexity.

- **Origin character:** Atitlán's microclimate and volcanic soil produce defined citrus acidity with decent body — brighter and more articulate than lower-grown Guatemalans
- **Variety (Caturra + Catuai):** Caturra brings the citrus spark and aromatic lift; Catuai smooths the edges with mild caramel and gentle florals — a balanced pairing
- **Processing (Washed, Sun Dried):** Washed processing amplifies terroir clarity; sun drying can add a subtle sweetness compared to mechanical drying
- **84.25 score:** Solid specialty — clean sweetness and citrus balance are the win condition, not layered complexity
