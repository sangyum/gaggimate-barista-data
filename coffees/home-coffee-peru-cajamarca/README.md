## What to Expect

A classically structured washed Peruvian from Cajamarca — known for clean, balanced cups with mild acidity and good body. The jasmine and tangerine notes point to a lighter roast with aromatic brightness, while nutmeg adds a warm, spiced undertone that grounds the cup. At this roast level, the coffee rewards a gentle extraction approach.

- **Origin character (Cajamarca):** One of Peru's most productive highland regions — clayey soils and cool temperatures at 1650–2000m produce coffees with good structure and restrained, clean acidity. Not the fruit-bomb of an Ethiopian natural, but articulate and sweet when extracted right.
- **Variety (Caturra/Catuai):** Dense, high-grown beans that need adequate temperature and time to open up. Resistance is real — don't grind too coarse.
- **Processing (Washed):** Clean, transparent cup that shows origin character clearly. The bloom phase here isn't about fighting CO2 (the bag is past peak degassing) — it's about even puck saturation before pressure builds, which preserves the floral notes.

> **Freshness note:** Roasted Aug 5, 2026 — approximately 61 days off roast when first brewed (Oct 2026). Past the peak flavor window (14–28 days for light roasts). Jasmine and tangerine aromatics will be muted; expect more nutmeg/dried fruit character than a fresh bag would show. Extraction is still possible — dial for sweetness, not aromatic brightness.
