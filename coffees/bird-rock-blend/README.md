## What to Expect

A daily-drinker blend designed for breadth: Colombian brightness balanced against Sumatran body, with the roast pushed far enough to develop chocolatey sweetness. The "fudge" descriptor is your anchor — this isn't a fruit-forward single origin, it's a balanced cup with a sweet base, lifted by red apple acidity and given backbone by Sumatra's signature spice.

- **Colombia (Caturra + Castillo):** Classic balanced sweetness. Caturra brings clean acidity and clarity; Castillo adds a softer, rounder body. Together they form the "approachable" half of this blend.
- **Sumatra (Typica + Catimor) — washed:** Unusual choice. Wet-hulled Sumatra is typical and gives those big earthy/funky notes; washed Sumatra is cleaner, with less mud and more spice. This is where the "black pepper" descriptor comes from. Catimor can carry a bitter edge if pushed too hot.
- **Costco-distributed:** Roast date is unknown and the bag has likely been in the supply chain 1-3 months. Expect less CO2 (bloom profiles won't shine) and a need for slightly tighter grind. Reasonable expectation: a comfortable, sweet daily-drinker — not a clarity bomb.
