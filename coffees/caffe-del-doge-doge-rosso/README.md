## What to Expect

A traditional Venetian espresso blend built for simplicity, low acidity, and chocolate-forward comfort. The cold-blending method (each origin roasted separately, then mixed) means each cup carries hints of all four origins without any single one dominating. This is a coffee designed for classic Italian espresso pulls — syrup, chocolate, and body — not for bright/fruity nuance.

- **Blend character:** Brazilian base for body/nut/chocolate, Colombian for sweetness and roundness, Guatemalan for balance, Indian (likely Monsooned Malabar or similar) for low acidity and heavy body. Expect a comforting, familiar espresso profile — the "safe defaults" you enjoy: chocolate, caramel, nutty.
- **Italian roast style:** Medium-dark, oil just barely present. Low acidity is engineered — don't expect brightness. Sweetness and body are the levers to work.
- **Freshness:** 25 days off roast is prime for an Italian blend (they often peak 3–6 weeks off roast, unlike specialty light roasts). No CO2 penalty at this age.
