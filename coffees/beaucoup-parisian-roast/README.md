## What to Expect

A classic French-café-style espresso blend from Vancouver's Beaucoup Bakery — designed to anchor their coffee program with reliability and comfort. The chocolate/caramel/butter trifecta signals a well-developed, low-acidity medium-dark that should pull smoothly and reward consistency over complexity.

- **Roast level (Medium-Dark):** Expect muted acidity, full body, and flavors that lean into roast development rather than origin character. The sweet spot between approachable and intense.
- **Processing (presumed washed):** Clean baseline — no fruity funk or fermentation notes to manage. The butter note is likely a Maillard byproduct of the roast rather than process character.
- **Blend character:** Without single-origin traceability, expect stability over variability — this coffee should behave consistently bag to bag, making it a good daily driver to dial in once and trust.
