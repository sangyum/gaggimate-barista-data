## What to Expect

A high-altitude washed Colombian with classic regional character — the kind of coffee that rewards patience. One day off roast means CO2 is still very active; expect variable first shots and plan to grind significantly coarser until the bag rests.

- **Origin character (Colombia, Huila-likely):** Typically rich fruit-forward sweetness with clean structure. Grape and apple land in the red-fruit / crisp-apple range characteristic of high Huila lots — not aggressive acidity, more of a juicy brightness.
- **Processing (Washed):** Clean, articulate cup; the fruit comes from the bean, not fermentation. Well-suited to bloom extraction for even saturation and sweetness development.
- **Density/Altitude (1800-2000 masl):** Dense, hard beans grind finer than expected. At 1 day off roast the CO2 compounds this — start coarser than history suggests and tighten as the bag rests toward day 5-7.
