## What to Expect

A traditional Italian "forte" — designed for classic 9-bar Italian extraction, not third-wave expression. Robusta backbone gives thick crema, heavy body, bittersweet chocolate/cereal, and near-zero acidity. This is an Americano/aerocano workhorse bean, not a nuanced sipper.

- **Origin character:** Brazilian base for chocolate/nut sweetness; Indian and Vietnamese robusta for body, crema, and caffeine intensity. Low acidity across the board.
- **Robusta component:** Higher chlorogenic acids → bitter risk if over-extracted. Lower temp (88–90°C) and shorter contact prevent harshness.
- **Freshness (~60–90 days):** Well past prime for most coffees, but Italian robusta blends age far better than light single-origins — less CO2 to lose, more roast-derived flavors, stable oils. Expect muted crema and softer aromatics, but core cereal/chocolate character intact. If it tastes woody/papery, that's oxidation and no amount of dialing will fix it.
- **Aerocano suitability:** Excellent — robusta body survives 1:5 dilution where lighter single-origins wash out.
