## What to Expect

A comfort-zone winter blend: a Brazil base for body, cocoa, and nutty depth, with Colombia and Guatemala layering in caramelized sweetness and a gentle fruit lift. The plum note is the interesting part — likely from a natural-processed Brazil component — and it will be quiet behind the roast, showing up in the finish more than the first sip.

- **Blend character:** Very close in composition to Caffè del Doge Doge Rosso (BR/CO/GT + India), which settled at DF64 10.7 on a lever decline at 91°C — the closest anchor in the grind map.
- **Roast style:** Medium-dark means sweetness and body are the levers; acidity will be low. A declining-pressure profile keeps the roast notes from turning ashy late in the shot.
- **Freshness:** ~19 days off roast at first pull — past heavy degassing and in the peak window for a medium-dark blend.
